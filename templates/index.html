Business Insights

    Problem Statement

    Businesses collect sales and customer data but often cannot predict future sales, profit or which customers will leave.

    Objectives

    Predict sales and profit, find customers likely to churn, and present the results in a clear dashboard.

    Technology

    Python, Flask, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Chart.js.

    Machine Learning Models

    Random Forest Regressor for sales and profit. Random Forest Classifier for churn. Scores are measured on unseen test data.

    Dataset

    DEMO DATA generated for this project. It does not represent a real company. You can replace data/retail_data.csv with your own.

    Workflow

    User, web interface, Flask backend, data processing, ML models, prediction, dashboard.
